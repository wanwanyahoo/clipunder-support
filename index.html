A free video toolkit for iPhone and iPad: compress to an MB limit, generate and translate subtitles, separate vocals and music, make Live Photos and save full-resolution video frames. No account, ads, subscriptions or watermarks.

New tools in 1.1

  • Automatic subtitles and translation: Choose a video, generate subtitles, then open Preview & edit to correct text and timing. Check the picture or play a short preview before saving an SRT or exporting a video with subtitles. Translate the corrected text and choose original, translated or bilingual captions.
  • Translation languages: Translation requires iOS 18 or later and a supported language pair. Apple may request a one-time language download. If the app displays the language setup guide, download both languages in Settings → Apps → Translate → Languages, then return to ClipUnder.
  • Separate vocals and music: On iOS 18 or later, choose one audio file or a Photos video. Separate it, preview both tracks and save two stereo WAV files. The model is included; no account or model download is required.
  • Live Photos: Choose Video to Live Photo and trim to the moment you want. Create and save a Live Photo of up to 3 seconds, then choose it in system wallpaper settings on a supported device. ClipUnder does not set the wallpaper directly.
  • Video frames: Choose Extract video frames, step through the clip, select up to 20 frames and export full-resolution JPEGs.
  • Manual text, SRT and masks: Import an existing SRT file, add text, or drag a fixed rectangle on the preview. This is separate from automatic subtitle recognition and automatic face masking.

Common questions

  • The file won’t fit: Choose a higher cap, trim the clip, or disable audio. The cap applies to each video individually. One MB is 1,000,000 bytes.
  • A recipient cannot play the file: Choose H.264 and MP4 in More options.
  • Photos save was denied: Allow ClipUnder to add to Photos in system Settings, or export using Share.
  • The task was interrupted: Reopen the app and start compression for the remaining videos. Completed copies are kept.
  • Need more device space: Remove individual queue entries or clear the queue. Your original and exported videos are unchanged.
