Batch-compress videos to a custom MB limit, extract audio, make GIFs and save still frames on your iPhone or iPad.

Common questions

  • The file won’t fit: Choose a higher cap, trim the clip, or disable audio. The cap applies to each video individually. One MB is 1,000,000 bytes.
  • A recipient cannot play the file: Choose H.264 and MP4 in More options.
  • Photos save was denied: Allow ClipUnder to add to Photos in system Settings, or export using Share.
  • The task was interrupted: Reopen the app and start compression for the remaining videos. Completed copies are kept.
  • Need more device space: Remove individual queue entries or clear the queue. Your original and exported videos are unchanged.

Contact the developer

Report a problem or request help. Please include your iOS version, ClipUnder version and steps to reproduce. The issue page is public; leave out private media and personal details.
