Common questions

What should I say? Try the phrase card. A random pick appears whenever you open the app, without repeats until its 36-card deck runs out. Change the app or phrase language in Settings.

I cannot record: Allow microphone access in iOS Settings. Move closer to the microphone if the recording is almost silent. Disconnect an unwanted headset input.

How do I learn the backwards sound? Start at 0.5×, learn one third at a time, and enable loop. Tap Record my imitation when ready. Stopping your imitation starts the reveal.

My file is long: In Reverse audio, import through Files and select 0.5–60 seconds. Your original file is unchanged.

Can I keep or share my recordings? Save a round to Moments, then play, compare, rename or search. Share audio alone or an original-and-result comparison. Exports use the original speed. Export important audio before deleting the app.

What happens in the background? Recording ends and playback pauses. You can return safely. A cancelled reveal countdown waits for you to continue.

Contact the developer

Report a problem or request help. Please mention FlipSide, your iOS and app version, and the steps to reproduce. The issue page is public; leave out private recordings and personal details.
