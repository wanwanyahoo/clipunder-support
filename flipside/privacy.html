Last updated November 12, 2026.

Reverse Audio: FlipSide records and reverses audio on your iPhone or iPad. The app does not operate an audio server, require an account, collect analytics, display ads, or track you. We do not receive your recordings.

Microphone and imported audio

Microphone permission is used only to record the voice clips you start. Recording stops when the app enters the background. Audio imported through Files may be downloaded by iOS or your selected file provider. FlipSide processes only the excerpt you select and does not modify the source file.

Local storage and preferences

Unsaved takes are temporary session files. Saved moments contain the original clip, the reversed result, an optional title and reaction, and a creation date, stored in the app’s Documents folder. You can delete saved moments in the app or inspect its files through Files. Deleting the app removes its local recordings. iOS device or iCloud backups may include saved app data according to your backup settings.

Language preferences and the remaining phrase deck are saved in app-only preferences so that suggested phrases do not repeat until the deck runs out. The phrase library and synthesized sample recordings are included in the app and work offline.

Sharing

Only audio you choose to share is handed to the native iOS share sheet. Your selected destination handles that copy under its own privacy practices. Exported audio does not include analytics or tracking identifiers from FlipSide.

Support

You may contact the developer through the public issue page. Do not include private recordings or personal information there. Support reports you submit are handled by GitHub under its privacy policy.
