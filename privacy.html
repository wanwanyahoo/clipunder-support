Privacy policy

SupportPrivacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026.

ClipUnder processes the videos you select on your device. The app does not operate a server, require an account, collect analytics, or track users. The app does not send selected videos to us.

Automatic speech recognition and vocals/instrumental separation use models included with the app and run on your device. Subtitle translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework. The system may download supported language packs. ClipUnder does not send your audio, video or subtitle text to a developer-operated service.

When you import a video from Photos or Files, the system may download it from your photo library or file provider. ClipUnder keeps a working copy and its export on the device until you remove the item or clear the queue. These copies are excluded from device backups. You can explicitly remove an original from Photos after compression. If you choose to replace it, Photos saves the compressed copy and removes the original in one library change. Exported copies follow the settings of their destination.

The separate vocals/instrumental workspace retains its current input and results locally so you can return to them. Choosing another input removes the previous local input and results; changing its time range removes the previous separation results. Saved or shared copies remain at the destination you selected.

Save to Photos uses add-only access. Browsing and filtering the library, choosing an album destination, and removing a Photos original require library access. The system picker remains available for selecting videos without granting library access. Save to Files copies completed outputs into the folder you select. Share hands selected files to the destination you choose. Exported files omit capture date, location and camera information by default. You can opt into keeping capture date and location when available, or keeping supported camera information in MOV exports.
